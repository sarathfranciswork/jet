## 1. TRADEMARK SEARCH

### Findings:

**Garrett Electronics "Z-Lynk" -- HIGH RISK**
- Garrett Electronics, Inc. (a major U.S. metal detector manufacturer) has an established product line called **"Z-Lynk"** -- their proprietary wireless audio technology for metal detectors. It is marketed with the trademark symbol (Z-Lynk(TM)) on product pages.
- Z-Lynk is used on multiple commercial products: wireless headphones, transmitters, receivers, and pinpointers.
- Garrett has an extensive patent portfolio and actively markets this technology.
- The Garrett URL path is literally `garrett.com/accessories/zlynk/` -- they use **"zlynk"** as a slug, treating it as an alternate spelling of "Z-Lynk."
- **Trademark class**: Garrett's products fall under Class 9 (electronic/scientific apparatus). A mobile game app would also be Class 9 (downloadable software). This is the **same trademark class**, which significantly increases infringement risk.
- eBay listings frequently spell it "Zlynk" or "Zlink" interchangeably with "Z-Lynk" (see eBay listing titles like "Garrett Z-Lynk... Zlink Zlynk").

**EZ Lynk SEZC -- MODERATE RISK**
- EZ Lynk SEZC (a Cayman Islands entity) holds registered trademarks including **"EZ LYNK DISPLAY"** (Serial No. 90850785) in Class 9 (Computer & Software Products). They make automotive diagnostic cloud-connected hardware/software.
- The "LYNK" element in the mark overlaps with "Zlynk."
- EZ Lynk has been involved in federal litigation (United States v. EZ Lynk, SEZC, S.D.N.Y.), so they are litigious.

**Confidence level: HIGH** that using "Zlynk" carries trademark risk due to Garrett's "Z-Lynk" in the same class.
